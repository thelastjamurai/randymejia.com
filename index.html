How I work

Growth is rarely about one lever. The real work is balancing price, promotion, mix, pack architecture, and trade against each other, often while there's pressure for results now. I connect the levers through rigorous analytics, then make the thinking practical, so teams can weigh the trade-offs and act with clarity and confidence.

The five levers

Price
Setting prices that reflect the value of the product and hold up with shoppers and retailers.
Promotion
Using promotions where they truly drive growth, and pulling back where they don't.
Mix
Putting the right packs in the right places, so shoppers find what they're looking for and retailers carry products that serve them.
Price pack architecture
Designing the sizes, formats, and price points that give every shopper a fair, clear choice.
Trade
Making trade investment work harder for retail customers and for the company.
